# Corporate group

Synthetic exercise — Asteria Maritime Insights.

Section D03 (Corporate group):
Halcyon Maritime Group, SYN-G01, is the parent of Meridian Shelf Shipping (SYN-O01) and of Bluewater Reach Logistics (SYN-O02) throughout 2026. Copper Bay Carriers (SYN-O03) is independent in this fictional register and has no parent company recorded. A corporate parent relationship does not make the parent the operator or the owner of the vessels its subsidiaries operate.
